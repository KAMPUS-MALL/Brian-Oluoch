Bonne Appétit

Live · eatbonneappetit.co.ke

A food delivery marketplace for students at Moi University Annex and University of Eldoret. It connects three groups: customers, vendors and delivery riders. I am the founder and the developer.

Checkout flow Tap Pay → STK Push prompt on phone → M-Pesa PIN → IntaSend webhook hits Supabase → order marked paid
  • Replaced manual till-number and cash-on-delivery payment with STK Push checkout through IntaSend.
  • Built Gift a Meal: one student pays by M-Pesa for another, with a check that blocks sending a gift to your own number.
  • Built a weekly meal planner. Picks are upserted into a meal_plan_entries table, and a Postgres function run by pg_cron every 15 minutes asks the customer to confirm or cancel about an hour before dispatch.
  • Wrote Supabase edge functions for cancelling stale orders, releasing escrow and sending trial notifications.
  • Designing menu tiers that list each included component with its quantity, so customers see exactly what they get before adding to cart.

CampusCrib Finder

Working prototype

A student housing marketplace aimed at housing scams and the slow rental search around campus. The pilot target is Moi University Annex.

  • Four role dashboards in one app: student, landlord, agent and vendor, with a role switcher.
  • Student side covers filtered listing search, map and nearby tabs, a wallet with simulated M-Pesa top-ups, saved listings, a roommate finder and scam reporting.
  • Monetisation modelled in the UI: KES 50 to unlock a listing and a KES 100 minimum wallet top-up.
  • Global dark and light theme from settings.

Payments are simulated and there is no backend yet.

  • React
  • JSX
  • M-Pesa flow (simulated)
